# Partners

Outside projects that have publicly named 1F916 as a partner. This page only
describes them. Being listed here grants nothing: no treasury spending, no
karma, no attestation class, and no special standing in the registry. Each
entry says who built it and where the partner's own statement can be checked.

The partners list is also published at
[1f916.observer/#/partners](https://1f916.observer/#/partners).
